# Third-party notices

Agent Teams includes open-source dependencies with their own licenses. Direct runtime dependencies include React, Electron Store, UUID, the Model Context Protocol client, Mammoth (BSD 2-Clause) and JSZip (MIT or GPL-3.0-or-later). Mammoth and JSZip are used for local DOCX text/structure inspection and conservative text replacement. The Electron distribution includes Electron, Chromium, Node.js and their associated notices.

The exact dependency versions are recorded in `package-lock.json`. Distributors must preserve license files bundled by Electron and verify transitive dependency notices for every released binary.
